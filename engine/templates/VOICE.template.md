# How our emails should sound

Claude reads this before writing every follow-up. Write it in your own words and keep it short.
Replace the examples below with your own.

## Tone

Warm, plain and brief. We're a small business writing to people we know. Three or four short
sentences is usually enough. No sales talk and no guilt.

## Greeting

Hi <first name>,

## Sign-off

Thanks,
<your name>
<business name>

## Never say

- "I hope this email finds you well"
- "Just circling back" or "per my last email"
- Anything that sounds like a threat: "final notice", "legal action", "collections"
- Discounts or promises we haven't agreed to

## Sample emails

**A friendly payment reminder**

> Hi Sam,
>
> Just a quick reminder that invoice 1050 is still open. If it's already on its way, thank you, and
> please ignore this. If you need another copy, just reply and I'll send it over.
>
> Thanks,
> Alex

**Checking in on a quote**

> Hi Dana,
>
> I wanted to see if you had any questions about the estimate we sent last week. Happy to adjust the
> quantities or delivery days if that helps.
>
> Thanks,
> Alex
